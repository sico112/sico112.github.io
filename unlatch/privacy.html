The short version. Unlatch has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your progress is saved only on your phone. The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for a bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before any personalised ads, and you can change that choice at any time.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the mobile game Unlatch (the "Game") and this website. The Game is developed and published by Sico Games ({{YOUR_LEGAL_NAME}}), {{COUNTRY}} ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the limited processing described here. You can reach us at sicogamingstudio@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Game progress (stays on your device)

The Game saves your level, coins, collected animals and treasures, and settings such as sound on or off in a file on your device. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its storage.

2.2 Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any account details. The Game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

2.3 Information collected by our advertising partner

The Game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). AdMob is used only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad, for example to get more undos, a hint, an extra cage or double coins. To deliver, measure and protect ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were watched or tapped;
  • diagnostic information, such as performance data and crash logs of the ad SDK.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

2.4 Purchases

If the Game offers optional in-app purchases, they are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We receive only the information needed to deliver what you bought, such as a product ID, an order ID and a purchase token. Google handles your payment under the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

2.5 Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We keep that correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request, and for at most 2 years.

4. Your choices about ads

  • You decide when to watch. The Game has no banner ads and no forced ads. An ad plays only when you tap a button that offers a reward.
  • Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the Game shows Google's consent message the first time it starts. You can accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change your choice later from the Game's menu.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads (the name varies by phone). After that, apps cannot use it for personalised ads.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it except as described here: with Google, as our advertising partner and as the operator of Google Play; or where the law requires it. Google may transfer data outside your country, including to the United States. For transfers from the EEA or UK, Google relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long data is kept

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies. Emails are kept for at most 2 years. Purchase records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

7. Children

The Game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. In the EEA it is not directed at children below the age of digital consent in their country, which is between 13 and 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold game data on our servers, most requests can be met by uninstalling the Game, resetting your advertising ID, or changing your ad consent in the Game. For anything else, email us at sicogamingstudio@gmail.com and we will answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell personal information. Google's ad services may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CCPA/CPRA. You can opt out by deleting your advertising ID or by refusing personalised ads in the Game.

9. Security

Your progress is stored in the Game's private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it. Data sent by the ad SDK is encrypted in transit by Google.
